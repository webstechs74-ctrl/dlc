About Devika Life Care

Nature Heals, We Guide.

Our story

Devika Life Care began with a simple observation. Around us, housewives and working women spend their days caring for everyone else, and their own health quietly slips down the list. Meals are skipped, sleep is short and small problems are ignored until they grow. Over time, this neglect affects energy, mood and overall well-being.

After research and many conversations, we decided to create wellness products designed with these women in mind: simple to use, rooted in Ayurveda and honest about what they do. That decision became Devika Life Care, a company built on the belief that every woman deserves to feel healthy, happy and strong.

Today we offer a curated range that includes Sea Buckthorn Juice, Green Tea Tablets and Vita Core+ multivitamin capsules, along with personal health consultation. Each product is chosen for its natural ingredients and everyday usefulness, and presented without exaggerated promises.

Our mission

Our mission is to empower people to live healthier, happier and more balanced lives through the wisdom of nature. We want Ayurveda to feel approachable: clear labels, fair prices and guidance in plain language, so you can make confident choices for yourself and your family.

We believe true health is not just the absence of disease, but a state of complete physical, mental and spiritual well-being.
